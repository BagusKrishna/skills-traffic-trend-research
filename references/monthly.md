# Monthly scan

Use for monthly, bulanan, this month, or a 30-day range. This is the default opportunity-research mode when the user gives no period.

- `this month/bulan ini`: the first day of the current local month through now; compare matching elapsed dates when possible.
- `last month/bulan lalu`: the previous complete calendar month.
- `last 30 days/monthly` without calendar wording: 30 complete local days ending yesterday; disclose any different provider window.
- Add a 12–24 month view for durability and seasonal context when available.

Combine search trends with estimated keyword demand, social questions/use cases, and direct competitors. A useful starting set is Google Trends Explore, Keyword Planner or an available SEO dataset, a relevant social/community source, and competitor pages/Similarweb. If paid tools cannot be accessed, proceed with available sources and mark absolute volume/difficulty as unavailable.

Group keywords by the same user job rather than by superficial wording. Keep country, language, match variants, and the volume estimate's averaging period visible. A 12-month average search volume is not the observed volume for this month. Examine why people search: to reach a brand, learn a fact, compare options, or complete a task that needs a tool.

Audit the strongest concepts for a specific functional gap and plausible recurring use. Describe one MVP workflow and distribution experiment per top concept. Consider whether the workflow can work without costly per-use APIs, but do not assume client-side processing is always feasible.

Finish with a ranked shortlist, evidence quality, gaps to validate, and separate recommendations for search acquisition and retention when relevant.

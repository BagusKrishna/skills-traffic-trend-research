# Yearly scan

Use for yearly, annual, tahunan, year in search, or a named year.

- A named completed year means January 1–December 31 of that year, subject to each report's actual cutoff.
- `this year/tahun ini`: January 1 through the current local date; compare the same elapsed dates in the previous year.
- `last 12 months/yearly` without calendar wording: 12 completed calendar months ending before the current month, unless the user/source specifies a rolling daily window; show the exact range.
- For seasonal subjects inspect at least two annual cycles when data is available.

Use Google Year in Search, long-range Google Trends charts, original annual platform/industry reports, and keyword/competitor trend histories. Open methodology pages for the main datasets. Annual reports published this year may describe the prior year; label the data period separately from publication date.

Google Year in Search identifies fast-growing searches for its stated window. It does not provide the largest annual search counts. Never use a prior year's list as evidence of this year's growth without newer data. If the requested current year is incomplete or its report is unreleased, use available year-to-date data and disclose the gap.

Separate structural changes, repeat seasonal demand, and one-off stories. For app opportunities, evaluate the recurring task, durable acquisition routes, competitive moat, and maintenance burden over a year. Explain which concepts survive outside their peak season.

Deliver durable opportunity themes with dated supporting evidence, seasonal timing, and a narrow experiment to test before investing in a full product.
